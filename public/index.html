Connect an AI assistant

Ask Claude (or another assistant that supports connectors) to add, schedule and check off quests for you, right from a conversation.

First, turn on sync above. Your assistant connects to your synced quests.

  1. In your assistant, open its connector settings. In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Paste this address as the connector URL:
  3. Choose Connect. When Quest Log asks, paste your sync code.
  4. Try it: "Add a quest to drink a glass of water at 3pm."

Your sync code unlocks your account, so only paste it into Quest Log's own page. Custom connectors may need a paid plan with your assistant.

Privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026. Plain language, no fine print.

The short version

  • Quest Log works without an account. Unless you turn on Sync, everything stays in your browser on your device.
  • If you turn on Sync, your check-ins, notes and moments are encrypted on your device before they leave it. The server stores them but can't read them.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing your data. Ever.
  • You can download all of it, or delete it from the server, at any time.

Without Sync

Your quests, check-ins, notes, loot, photos, songs, videos and recordings are stored in your browser's own storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. If you clear your browser's data or lose the device, it's gone, so use Settings → Back up now and then.

With Sync

Sync gives you a long, random sync code. It works like a key: anyone who has it can open your data, so keep it private.

  • Encrypted on your device, unreadable to the server: check-ins (mood, anxiety, energy, body, focus, sleep and pain notes, pictures), your notes from past you, and moments (captions, titles, links, and the photo, video, song and recording files themselves). They're sealed with AES-256-GCM using a key made from your sync code, which never leaves your device.
  • Readable by the server, so features work: quests (names, tiny steps, schedules), XP, loot, badges and the days you were active. Push reminders need quest times, and the Claude connector needs quests.
  • The server knows your account only by a one-way hash of your sync code, not the code itself.
  • If you lose your sync code, nobody, including us, can unlock the encrypted parts. Keep a copy somewhere safe.

If you connect Claude

The Quest Log connector lets Claude read and change your quests and log check-ins for you. A check-in Claude logs sits on the server unencrypted only until your device next syncs; then it's sealed like the rest. Claude can't read your older, encrypted check-ins, notes or moments.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and stores synced data and backed-up files (encrypted as described above).
  • Google Fonts serves the app's typefaces, so Google sees your IP address when the fonts load. No other Google services are used.
  • Your phone's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) wakes the app when a reminder is due, if you turn reminders on. It only gets a wake-up signal; the app then fetches the reminder's text from our server.
  • Anthropic, only if you connect Claude yourself.

Your controls

  • Download everything: Settings → Back up.
  • Delete from the server: Settings → Delete my data from the server. This removes your synced data, reminders and backed-up files from the server, and turns Sync off. Your device keeps everything.
  • Delete anything one at a time: check-ins, notes and moments can each be deleted in Journey.

Health information

Quest Log is a personal tool, not a medical service, and it doesn't replace care from a professional. It isn't connected to any doctor, insurer or health system. If something ever went wrong with the security of your data, we'd tell you promptly.
